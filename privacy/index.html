Privacy Policy for Outwave

Outwave is a Telegram bot that converts office documents to PDF.

What we process: files you send to the bot, only to convert them. To do this, files are temporarily uploaded to Google Drive via the Drive API, converted, and deleted immediately after the PDF is produced.

What we store: nothing. We do not keep your files, the converted PDFs, or their contents. We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Third parties: conversion is performed by Google Drive, subject to Google’s privacy policy.

Contact: outwave.labs@gmail.com
